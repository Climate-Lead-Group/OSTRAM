Applications

An application is a setup: a manifest in the repository that names every input of one model. The pipeline, the rules and this site are shared; the data are not. Every setup in the setup registry gets a page here.

{{block:setup_list}}

The training setup is a reduced model used in the OSTRAM training. It shares the framework and is listed for completeness.

Adding an application

A new region needs its own manifest, inputs and scenario registry, and an entry in config/setups/registry.json. Once registered, its page, its headline counts on the home page and its rows in the transmission pages fill themselves.
